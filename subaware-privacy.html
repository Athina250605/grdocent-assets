Privacy Policy for SubAware

SubAware is a subscription tracking application that helps users manage and understand their recurring expenses.

Information Collection

SubAware does not collect personal information. All subscription data entered by the user is stored locally on the user's device.

Data Usage

The information entered into the app is used only for calculating subscription costs, tracking recurring payments, and providing spending insights to the user.

Data Storage

All subscription data is stored locally on the user's device. SubAware does not store user data on external servers.

Data Sharing

SubAware does not share, sell, or transmit user data to third parties.

Security

User data remains on the user's device and is not transmitted externally.

Children’s Privacy

SubAware is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future. Any updates will be posted on this page.
