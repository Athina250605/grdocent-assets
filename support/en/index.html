Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-16

Greece Docent (the “App”) values your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The app can be used without registration and does not directly collect your personal information.

  • Automatically collected technical information: The app currently does not use separate analytics tools and does not collect non-identifiable technical information.
  • Payments: If paid features are offered, payments are processed through Apple/Google payment systems. The app does not store or process your card information directly.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Providing app functions and operating the service
  • Maintaining app stability and improving errors
  • Responding to user inquiries (when you contact us by email, etc.)

3. Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not directly store personal information, there is generally no separate retention or deletion process. If you provide information by email for support, it will be deleted without delay after the inquiry is resolved.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not provide your personal information to third parties.

  • Apple/Google payment systems: used for in-app payment processing
  • Content delivery servers: external servers are used to provide audio and image content, and technical request information such as IP addresses may be processed temporarily in that process.

5. Location Information

The app may use location information to provide GPS-based features. Location information is processed only on your device and is not stored on or collected by the server.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time regarding privacy matters. If the app does not directly store personal information, the scope of possible requests may be limited.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact:

  • Email: contact.greecedocent@gmail.com
  • Developer: Independent Developer

9. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated, and any changes will be announced on this page.
